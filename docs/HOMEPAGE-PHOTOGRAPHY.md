## Selection

- Opening image: Atithi, Scarborough / `_Z7A8917.jpg`. Warm timber, black upholstered booths, lighting and the branded service counter appear together. The professional landscape photograph presents the delivered interior and suits Terranode's warm material palette. It was selected over the Jimmy John's kitchen views, crowded opening-day photographs and smaller phone images. Pita Pit photos were not found in the retrieved source folder.
- Closing image: Jimmy John's, Windsor / `IMG_5118.JPG`. The red brand lettering, black tile, front counter, menu boards and pickup area are visible. Its caption names the service counter and interior finishes rather than making a generic statement about a completed restaurant interior.
- Both are attributed to prior work under LifeBuild Canada. No new project or scope claim was added.

## Presentation

The previous closing photograph was enlarged to 240%, with a bottom-biased crop; that concealed most of the restaurant context. This zoom and its hover animation were removed. The replacement uses a simple cover crop, with a separate phone focal position. The hero keeps the established frame, caption treatment and Terranode stamp; its phone crop favours the branded counter.

The original photos were resized into progressive JPEG variants for the website, with no retouching, generated content or colour alteration. Hero widths: 800 and 1600 pixels. Closing widths: 600 and 1200 pixels. All variants are below the existing 400 KB image budget. Original filenames, Drive references, dimensions and export sizes are recorded in `project-sources.json`.
