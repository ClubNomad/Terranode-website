# Human voice and enquiry copy review

Reviewed 2026-10-09 against the latest merged Terranode website.

## What changed

- The About introduction now invites clients to share what they know and what is still uncertain. It keeps the LifeBuild Canada attribution for prior projects.
- Manjil's description focuses on scope, estimates, schedules, trade coordination and work on site. Dipesh's uses a more conversational rhythm and focuses on listening to clients, sourcing, operations and day-to-day decisions. Both roles and their direct involvement were already described on the site; no personal story or qualification was added.
- The approach and three first steps use shorter, more concrete language. The project enquiry page explains how the note is reviewed, how a conversation begins and that an enquiry is not a commitment to start work.
- Project page invitations welcome early ideas and questions without adding urgency or a promised response time.

## Basis for the writing

- Nielsen Norman Group, [Concise, Scannable, and Objective](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/concise-scannable-and-objective-how-to-write-for-the-web/): use short, scannable, non-promotional text.
- UK Office for National Statistics, [Plain language](https://service-manual.ons.gov.uk/content/writing-for-users/plain-language): put users' needs first; use clear headings, simple language and action-oriented calls to action.

The changes apply those principles by naming who clients speak with, describing the first three steps and making the next action clear. They do not use urgency, testimonials or unverified claims.

## Review and boundaries

- Reviewed the homepage About, approach, process and invitation together with the project enquiry and four project endings. Existing project evidence, themes, layout, forms, SEO and social-sharing metadata were preserved.
- The site checker passes. The revised sections were visually reviewed at 390px and 1440px; six changed pages reflow without horizontal overflow at 320px with 200% text.
- Form destinations, original field mappings, canonical tags and social metadata were compared with the previous version and are unchanged.
- Google Form delivery and internal notifications were not retested here. No reply deadline is promised. If the company wants a precise response timeframe, confirm that operational commitment before adding one.

No personal detail was essential to make this pass. An optional future refinement is one genuine detail from each director about how they like to work with clients, written in their own words.
